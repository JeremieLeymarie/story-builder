# Story game

If you click on the "play" button you see the first scene of the story

<img src="/img/library_games_choices.png" alt="logo" width="600" />

Proceeding here is quite simple. As you already know, Story Builder is a "Build your own story" kind of game, as such you have to make choices along
your adventures which lead you into different paths of the game, the choices you make then can lead to yet another path and so on and on.

Ok so now let's chose the second option "You head east towards Glitterlake".
You reach another scene with new choices that take you further away in the story line.
Progressing down the story is quite simple that way, all you have to do is let your choices dictate the way the story unfolds.

<img src="/img/library_games_progression.png" alt="logo" width="600" />

You can see that each time you progress in the story the percentage of progression goes up and the graph overview gets updated accordingly.
