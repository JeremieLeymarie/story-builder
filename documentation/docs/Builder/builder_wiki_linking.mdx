# Wiki linking

Story Builder offers to their users the possibility to enrich the lore of their story even further.
As such there is a possibility to link any word or sentence present on a scene description field to an existing wiki.

Of course you need to have an already existing wiki to link it into your scene.
Let's make a demonstration here !

Let's say that i want to link a wiki on the third scene of my story, all i need is to go to the toolbar on the upper left side of your screen and click on the "Edit story" button.

<img src="/img/builder_tools_edit_story.png" alt="Builder tools edit story" width="500" />

A window appears on the right side of your screen, you just need to select an existing wiki on the last field the form entitled "Linked wiki" :

<img src="/img/builder_tool_story_popup.png" alt="Builder tool story popup" width="900" />

You the select and existing wiki:

<img src="/img/builder_tool_wiki_selection.png" alt="Builder tool wiki selection" width="500" />

Here we choose the "Story Builder" Wiki.
After that we select our third scene and we highlight a word or a sentence in this case the word "word", we then click on the "Wiki" button and select the wiki:

<img src="/img/builder_tool_wiki_adding.png" alt="Builder tool wiki adding" width="500" />

If you want to consult the wiki after that all you have to is hover your mouse above it and click on the wiki link.
