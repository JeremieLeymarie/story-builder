# Characters

In Story Builder you can configure characters in the edit charcter section of the builder tools.
To access it you just have to click on the "Edit Character" button of the builder tools.

<img src="/img/builder_tool_character_edit.png" alt="builder tool character edit" width="700" />

This opens up a window on the right side of your screen titled "Edit character", you need to click on the "+" button on the top to add a new character.

<img src="/img/builder_tool_character_setting.png" alt="builder tool character setting" width="700" />

Here we name our charatcer "Bob" and give him an initial value of 3. Value is a "stats" kind of feature for your characters that will allow you the drive your story even further by playing
with this initial value that can be modified through the "Side effects" tab on any scene panel.

<img src="/img/builder_scene_side_effects.png" alt="builder scene side effects" width="400" />

In this eight scene we can upgrade Bob's initial value from 3 to 4 just by accessing this scene and by choosing the seventh action on the fourth scene that leads to this eight scene.
You can also retrieve a point for that you just have to switch from add to retrieve :

<img src="/img/builder_tool_character_minus.png" alt="builder tool character minus" width="400" />

You can see that this a very interesting possibilities for story development as you tweak the values at any given time, that will allow the player the power to do some actions that would have been impossible
if they did not have the required amount of character value.

If you combine character side effects with conditional actions and random events you can create very intricate stories with compelling story telling.

Lastly it is important to note that you can create more than one character as displayed in the image at the beginning of the page, all you have to do is click on the "+" button to create another character.
